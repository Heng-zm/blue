# MGS v0.2.2

Image sending & inline previews, accelerated BLE transmission, and real-time transfer speed & ETA.

## Changes in this release

- **Send images & photos**: Added a dedicated photo picker button in the chat input bar (`Icons.photo_outlined`) allowing direct selection of images from your gallery or camera roll.
- **Inline image previews**: Images received or sent in chat now render with smooth, rounded inline thumbnail previews and tap-to-open support.
- **Accelerated BLE transfers**: Optimized GATT packet transmission to utilize `writeWithoutResponse` when supported by the peer link, eliminating write-acknowledgement roundtrip bottlenecks for significantly faster blob throughput.
- **Tuned buffer throughput**: Increased blob sender high-water mark buffer to 128 KiB and tightened capacity polling delay to 10 ms for sustained high data rates.
- **Real-time transfer speed & ETA**: Both outgoing and incoming file transfers now calculate and display live transfer speed (e.g., `120 KB/s`, `1.4 MB/s`) and estimated completion time (e.g., `ETA 12s`, `ETA 1m 05s`) alongside the progress bar.
